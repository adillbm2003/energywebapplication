# Image credits

Bermuda Department of Energy — provenance for images shipped in
`portal/public/images/`.

This file exists because a watermarked stock photo reached production: the
Space & Satellite banner carried a visible *pngtree* watermark and appeared in
six places on the live site, including the top of its own section page. A
watermark is evidence the image was never licensed. Anything added here should
have a source that can be named.

## Replaced, September 2026

| File | Source | Licence |
|------|--------|---------|
| `space-satellites.jpg` | NASA JSC, `iss059e104296` (17 June 2019) — three CubeSats from Nepal, Sri Lanka and Japan ejected from the Kibo module's Small Satellite Orbital Deployer. Cropped to 16:9 from the original 5568×3712. | NASA media, public domain |
| `space-insurance.jpg` | NASA Blue Marble, western hemisphere. Composed onto 1280×720 so the 16:9 card shows the globe whole. | NASA media, public domain |

NASA still images are generally not copyrighted and may be used without
requesting permission. The exceptions are the NASA insignia and identifiable
individuals, neither of which appears in these two.

## Known problems, not yet resolved

- `space-launch.jpg` and the former `space-insurance.jpg` are photographs of a
  named commercial launch provider, with its wordmark legible. `space-launch.jpg`
  is not displayed anywhere; it should be replaced before it ever is.
- `space-dish.jpg` is labelled as a ground-station dish but is a close-up of
  someone soldering a circuit board, with two component brands legible. It is
  shown on the Earth Stations & Operations card.
- The provenance of the remaining bundled photographs is unrecorded. Absence of
  a visible watermark is not evidence of a licence.
